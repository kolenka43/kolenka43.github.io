Privacy Policy — YT Planlayici

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. What the app is

YT Planlayici is a desktop application that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to schedule when they are published. It is not offered to the public and has no server.

2. Data the app accesses

  • With your permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to read basic information about your YouTube channel (channel name, ID, upload playlist, your playlists) and to upload videos, set thumbnails, add videos to playlists and check the scheduled publish time.
  • The app uses the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube only for these purposes.

3. Where data is stored

Google access tokens, settings and upload logs are stored only on the user's own computer (in the Windows user data folder). Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The app sends data only to Google/YouTube, and only to perform the actions the user requests.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, share, transfer or use your data for advertising. Data is not used to train any models.

5. Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used; by using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a channel in the app deletes its stored token from the computer. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes all stored data.
